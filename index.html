NEXT VALIDATION

Move from diagrams to measured tests.

The next useful step is not a bigger claim. It is a smaller, instrumented experiment with visible assumptions and repeatable outputs.

  1. Freeze a requirements matrix.
  2. Instrument braking repeatability on a benchtop guideway.
  3. Measure mass, power and thermal load.
  4. Review evacuation and loss-of-power failure modes.
  5. Seek independent engineering and medical-device review.
